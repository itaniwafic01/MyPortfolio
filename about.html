About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at AUB, concentrating in Dynamics, currently on exchange at Lund University in Sweden. My focus is vehicle performance and powertrain engineering — building simulation models, analysing real-world data, and using both to drive decisions that make vehicles faster and more efficient.

I work at the intersection of engineering fundamentals and data-driven analysis. My projects span CFD validation, energy recovery system modelling, and an ML pipeline that predicted the 2026 Austrian GP qualifying order blind, before the session ran. I follow Formula 1 and Formula E closely — not as a fan, but as an engineer looking at the gap between what the data says and what the car does.

I am actively seeking an industrial placement in motorsport or high-performance automotive engineering for 2027–2028, where I can contribute to performance analysis, simulation, and the kind of work that feeds real decisions at the track.
